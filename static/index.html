THE THINKING BEHIND IT

Simple enough to question.

01 / INPUT

Start with honest data

The built-in facility readings are generated examples. Upload your own continuous hourly CSV to work with measured values. No numbers are presented as real grid readings.

02 / TRAINING

Respect the timeline

The model sees earlier hours, never future demand. The most recent input for each target hour comes from at least 24 hours earlier. The final block is held back for evaluation.

03 / REALITY CHECK

Keep the benchmark

A more complex model is not always better. We compare error with a seasonal-naive forecast and show when our model performs worse, not just when it wins.

↗
